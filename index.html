Privacy Policy for UPSC Prelims PYQ Prep

Effective Date: 12 November, 2026 | Developer: PRANU Developers

1. Introduction & Scope

UPSC Prelims PYQ Prep ("the Application"), developed by PRANU Developers ("we", "us", or "our"), provides an offline-first mobile practice platform for candidates preparing for competitive Civil Services Examinations. We are committed to maintaining transparent data practices and safeguarding user privacy.

2. Zero Personal Identity Collection

We do not collect, request, trade, or store Personal Identification Information (PII). We do not prompt for names, email addresses, phone numbers, location data, or contact lists.

3. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Application integrates Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect non-personally identifiable technical information, such as your device's Advertising ID (AAID) and system logs, to serve non-personalized or personalized ads in compliance with Google's Advertising Policies.

4. On-Device Local Data Storage

All practice statistics, test scores, mistake history, and bookmarked questions are stored locally on your physical smartphone storage via SQLite. We do not upload your test results to remote servers.

5. Children's Privacy

The Application is intended for competitive examination candidates aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

6. Government Non-Affiliation Disclaimer

UPSC Prelims PYQ Prep is an independent educational study tool developed by PRANU Developers. This application is NOT affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to the Union Public Service Commission (UPSC) or the Government of India.
